# Story Studio

A private studio for a faceless storytelling channel. It turns a written story into finished, narrated videos, ready to upload.

## What it does

- **Write once, publish everywhere.** One script becomes a full landscape video, a full vertical video and short vertical teasers.
- **Voices for every character.** Each character gets a distinct voice, chosen from a cast you can preview.
- **Illustrated scenes.** Every scene gets an image in one consistent house style, or your own artwork.
- **Captions and music.** Captions are burned in, background music is mixed under the voices, and loudness is levelled for streaming platforms.
- **Live preview while writing.** Scenes, characters, issues and estimated length update as you type.
- **Free-tier aware.** Built-in meters show how much of each allowance is used, with storage management per story.
- **Private by default.** The whole app sits behind a login.
